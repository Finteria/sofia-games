# Sofia's games

Small learning games for Sofia (3½), in Russian and Latvian, made to run in Safari on an iPad.

- `index.html` – game menu
- `jungle/` – София в джунглях: tap the bush, meet an animal, hear its sound and its name. There are 20 animals (jungle and farm); each game picks 6 at random. After each animal a red mic button appears next to Sofia: one tap records her for 3 seconds and plays it straight back (nothing is saved).

- `letters/` – Буквы · Burti: Russian or Latvian letters (one alphabet per game, each with its own colour). Three balloons float up, a picture pops up in Sofia's speech bubble and she says its name, then asks for its letter: «Арбуз! Где буква А?» / «Apelsīns! Kur ir burts A?». Tapping the right balloon pops it and shows a card with the letter, the picture and the word («А — арбуз!», «S — Sofija!» with Sofia's own face). Pictures are everyday things a small child knows; the family's names (Амелия, Вероника, Ваграм, Тата, София) are extra choices for their letters. Where no word starts with the letter, the word has it inside (Ы — сыр, Ь — конь, Ļ — bļoda). After a quiet pause the right balloon starts to shake a little as a hint. A wrong tap wobbles the balloon and Sofia asks again; after two wrong tries, or 14 seconds without a tap, the right balloon glows. Tapping the bubble or the speaker asks again. A letter she mixed up comes back later in the same game. Look-alike letters (Е/Ё, И/Й, A/Ā, S/Š…) never appear in the same round. 10 letters per game, then a sticker page. «Азбука» / «Ābece» opens every letter with its picture and word. Pictures are emoji. Letter data is the `RU`/`LV` arrays at the top of the script; a letter can have several words (В: вишня, Вероника, Ваграм), one is picked at random each round.
- `songs/` – Песенки · Dziesmiņas: picture karaoke on a little stage. Five traditional songs (Bingo, Old MacDonald, Finger Family, Baby Shark, Twinkle Twinkle), in English. The words are shown as pictures with the words under them for grown-ups, a ball hops from word to word and Sofia sings along. In Old MacDonald, Finger Family and Baby Shark she picks who comes next from big pictures (farm animals make their real sounds from `jungle/audio/`; one finger is "Sofia finger"). In Bingo the letters turn into claps, one more each verse. A singing voice sings every verse with its words (`songs/voice/`, one MP3 per verse, made with SoulX-Singer; see `songs/voice/CREDITS.txt`), and the band plays live in the browser (Web Audio) in time with it. The voice files start 0.5 s before the first note; if one is missing, that verse plays with the band only. 🎤 records her singing during the song and plays it back at the end; the recording stays on the device and is never uploaded. Songs are the `SONGS` table at the top of the script: words, notes, beats and chords per line.
- `colours/` – Colours · Цвета · Krāsas: English colour names while painting a picture. Three pictures (garden, beach, farm) in a frame, every thing in them grey at first (emoji that really have their colour: sun, apple, frog, crab, pig, sunflower, black cat…). One thing glows and Sofia says «The sun is yellow. Where is yellow?»; paint pots stand below the picture. The right pot throws a blob of paint, the thing turns its real colour and Sofia says «Yellow sun!». A wrong pot wobbles and says its own colour («Red. Where is yellow?»); after a quiet pause the right pot shakes a little, after two wrong tries or 15 seconds it glows. Two pots to begin with, three once she has found four colours on the first try (remembered on the device). Ten colours: red, yellow, blue, green, orange, purple, pink, brown, black, white. When the picture is done, everything in it dances; painted things say their name when tapped. Sofia talks with a child's voice (Microsoft's neural voice Ana; see `colours/audio/CREDITS.txt`). Colours and pictures are the `COLOURS`/`SCENES` data at the top of the script.
- `counting/` – Counting · Счёт · Skaitīšana: counting to ten in English. A round is five framed pictures (ducks on a pond, bees in the sky, crabs on the beach, fish in the sea, cows and chickens in a meadow…). Sofia says «Let's count the ducks!» and she taps each animal once: it hops, gets a number badge and Sofia says the number. A counted animal can't be counted twice. Then «How many ducks?» with two or three number cards (the digit and that many dots, in rows of five); the right card makes the animals dance and Sofia says «Three ducks!». A wrong card wobbles and Sofia counts again with her, each badge lighting up as she says its number. Quiet pauses bring a gentle hint (the next animal or the right card shakes). How far she counts grows by itself: 1–3, then 1–5, then 1–10 after five right answers on the first try (remembered on the device; a grown-up can pick it on the start screen). At the end every animal of the round comes back for a parade. «Numbers» shows 1 to 10 as tiles; tapping one shows that many animals and says it («Seven turtles!»). Sofia talks with a child's voice (Microsoft's neural voice Ana; see `counting/audio/CREDITS.txt`). Animals and places are the `ANIMALS` data at the top of the script.
- `peekaboo/` – Peekaboo · Ку-ку · Paslēpes: animals hide behind three bushes. A round is six animals. For the first three, one animal hides alone: Sofia says «Who's hiding? Listen!», the bush rustles, the ears peek over the top and the animal makes its real sound (the jungle game's recordings), again every few seconds. An empty bush lets a butterfly or ladybug flutter out («Not here!»). For the last three, three animals peek out at once and only one calls («Who is it? Listen!»); a wrong one pops up and calls too. The right bush: the animal jumps out, «Peekaboo! It's a cow! The cow says moo!», with the big celebration. After two misses or 16 seconds the right bush glows. Fourteen animals; Sofia talks with Microsoft's child voice Ana. Animals are the `ANIMALS` data at the top of the script.
- `feed/` – Feed the animals · Ням-ням! · Ņam-ņam!: a picnic in a meadow. On the start screen a grown-up picks the language: English, Russian or Latvian (each with its own voice). A hungry animal hops in («The bunny is hungry! What does the bunny eat?» / «Зайчик хочет кушать! Что любит зайчик?» / «Zaķītis grib ēst! Ko ēd zaķītis?») and the food stands on plates on a picnic blanket. She drags a food to the animal, or just taps it and it flies there; picking a food up says its name («A carrot!», «Морковка!», «Burkāns!»). The right food: three crunchy bites with crumbs, hearts, the big celebration, «Yum yum! The bunny loves carrots!» and the animal's real sound if the jungle game has one; then it hops away. The wrong food: the animal turns its head away, «No, thank you!», and the food goes back to its plate. After a quiet pause the right food wiggles; after two wrong tries or 16 seconds it glows. Twelve animals (cat–fish, dog–bone, bunny–carrot, monkey–banana, mouse–cheese, cow–grass, horse–apple, bear–honey, squirrel–nuts, chicken–corn, bird–worm, caterpillar–leaves), six per game, met in turns so all of them come up; a food the animal would also eat is never offered as a wrong one. Two plates to begin with, three once she has fed four animals on the first try (remembered on the device). At the end everyone she fed dances. Voices: Microsoft's neural voices Ana (English, a child's voice), Svetlana (Russian), Everita (Latvian); see `feed/audio/CREDITS.txt`. Animals and foods are the `ANIMALS`/`FOODS` data at the top of the script.
- `weather/` – Dress Sofia · Одёжка · Drēbītes: Sofia stands in her room by a big window that shows sun, rain or snow (the rain and snow move). A grown-up picks English, Russian or Latvian on the start screen. Sofia says the weather («It's raining!») and asks for one thing at a time («Something for my feet, for the puddles!»); a shelf holds three things, the right one and two from the other weathers. She drags one onto Sofia or taps it (picking it up says its name: «Rubber boots!»). The right thing goes on Sofia (drawn inside her cartoon, so it moves with her) with the big celebration; a wrong one: Sofia shakes her head, «Hmm, not this one!», and it goes back. Sunny: sunglasses, sun hat, sandals; rainy: raincoat, rubber boots, umbrella; snowy: warm hat, scarf, mittens. Hints as in the other games (a wiggle, then a glow). When she is dressed, Sofia goes outside and plays in that weather: butterflies in the sun, splashing in a puddle, a snowman in the snow. A game is all three weathers in random order. Voices: Microsoft's neural voices Ana (English), Svetlana (Russian), Everita (Latvian); see `weather/audio/CREDITS.txt`. The clothes are the `ITEMS` drawings at the top of the script, in the cartoon's own coordinates.
- `music/` – Музыка · Mūzika: a little music player with four songs about Sofia and her cats (Софа и котики, Софика и котики, two versions of each; made with Suno, see `music/audio/CREDITS.txt`). Tap a song's tile to play it, tap again to pause; ⏮ ⏭ switch songs and the bar seeks. Each song has its own cat on its cover, which dances while it plays, and the words being sung show under the title for grown-ups (the timings come from the lyrics inside the original files). When a song ends the next one starts, round and round. The last song picked is remembered on the device. On the iPad lock screen the songs show with their cover and can be paused and skipped. The page is built from `~/sofia-work/src/music/` (`prep.py` turns the m4a files into mp3 and `songs.json`, `build.py` writes the page).

Sofia walks through the jungle as a cartoon girl (inline SVG in `jungle/index.html`) drawn after her portrait `jungle/sofia.png`: round cheeks, side-swept bangs, a top-knot curling right. Her hair color is the `.hair` fill in the CSS.

## Family voices

The game speaks only with recorded family voices, no robot voice.

- On the start screen, press and hold the microphone button (top right) for about a second to open the recording page.
- The first time on a device, enter the family code (kept on the server in `family-key.txt`, see below).
- Record each word and each animal sound. Recordings are saved on the server, so clearing Safari data or switching devices doesn't lose them. A new take replaces the old one, but the old one is archived: «Вернуть» brings it back. «Удалить» archives too.
- Real animal sounds from Wikimedia Commons and Freesound are in `jungle/audio/` (`<animal>-sound.mp3`, credits in `jungle/audio/CREDITS.txt`); the peekaboo game plays them too. The snake and crocodile sounds are generated. A family recording of a sound replaces the real one in the jungle game. The jungle game meets its animals in turns (remembered on the device), so every animal comes up within a few games.
- Backup option: put audio files in `jungle/audio/`, named after the clip, e.g. `monkey-ru.m4a`, `monkey-lv.m4a`, `monkey-sound.m4a`, `greet-ru.m4a`, `end-lv.m4a`. A family recording takes priority over a file.

The letters game talks with a computer voice (Microsoft neural voices: Latvian Everita, Russian Svetlana; see `letters/audio/CREDITS.txt`). Holding the mic button on its start screen opens «Голоса для букв», where each phrase can be replaced by a family recording, stored on the same voice server (keys start with `abc-`).

## Voice server

`server/voices.py` (Python, standard library only) stores the recordings, and Caddy proxies `/api/*` to it. Anyone can listen, but saving, deleting and restoring need the family code. Data lives outside the repo: `current/<key>.<ext>`, `history/<key>/` (the last 30 older takes per clip) and `family-key.txt`.

```
docker network create sofia && docker network connect sofia <caddy container>
docker run -d --name sofia-voices --restart unless-stopped --network sofia --user 1000:1000 \
  -v $PWD/server:/app:ro -v ~/sofia-voices:/data python:3.12-alpine python /app/voices.py
```

Without the server (e.g. on GitHub Pages) the game still works with the sound files and generated sounds, but recording isn't possible.

## Publishing

On the iPad, open the site in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen.
